About

I initially joined a coding bootcamp to get a better job, but I ended up building a digital museum for dead websites. From setting up my first server to full-stack integration, I became more fascinated with the engineering side of it. Going beyond what was required of the bootcamp, I began connecting to the Wayback CDX API and making engineering decisions I had no roadmap for. I have made internet preservation more streamlined and community-based, because I wanted to preserve as much of the old internet that I grew up with. And honestly, that is not the thing I imagined I would do in my life.
